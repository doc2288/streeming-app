## 2026-06-23 - [Add ARIA labels to icon-only buttons]\n**Learning:** In frontend components, icon-only buttons like copy to clipboard, delete, emoji pickers, or hamburger menus lacked explicit aria-label attributes for screen-reader accessibility, relying sometimes only on a hardcoded title attribute.\n**Action:** Use localization strings with `t(...)` to provide standard `aria-label` and `title` tags for all icon-only buttons. Avoid hardcoding English or non-localized text.
